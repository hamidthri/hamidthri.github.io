Where **language models meet robot planning** — and a rigorous way to score them. The
benchmark poses planning problems in a PDDL-lite robot domain and checks the LLM's plans with
a strict validator.

## What it measures

- **Validity** — does the plan actually solve the task under the domain's constraints?
- **Optimality gap** — how far from the **oracle A\*** gold plan?
- **Language sensitivity** — does performance shift from English to German prompts?
- **Repairability** — can the model fix an invalid plan given a single clarifying question?

This is exactly the kind of evaluation-driven, robotics-flavored research question I want to
pursue at PhD level.
