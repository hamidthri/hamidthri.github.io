A disciplined backtesting environment grounded in the quant literature (Chan, López de Prado).
The emphasis is **methodology over curve-fitting**: a catalog of strategies, transaction costs
included in every backtest, a hard cap of ≤5 free parameters per strategy, and validation
designed to resist overfitting.
