A study of the **deep-learning toolkit for 3D perception**, built by reimplementing the
canonical architectures and benchmarking them head-to-head.

## Results

- **DGCNN** part segmentation on ShapeNetPart: **80.22% mIoU, 91.32% accuracy**.
- **PointNet++** classification on ModelNet10: **91.4% accuracy**.
- Clean, comparable implementations of PointNet, PointNet++, DGCNN and PointMLP, so the
  architectural differences — rather than training tricks — drive the numbers.

The image shows DGCNN segmentation predictions against ground truth: per-point part labels
recovered directly from raw 3D coordinates. This line of work connects directly to the
**drifting point-cloud field in the site's hero scene**.
