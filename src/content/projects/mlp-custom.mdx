A neural network with **no autograd and no framework** — just NumPy and the chain rule.

## What it does

- A configurable multilayer perceptron supporting **classification and regression**.
- Multiple **activation functions** and **MSE loss**, with forward and backward passes
  implemented by hand.
- Built to make the gradients concrete: every weight update is traceable to the math, which
  is the best way to really understand training.
