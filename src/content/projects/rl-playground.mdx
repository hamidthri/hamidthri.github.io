A consolidated **RL / imitation-learning** workspace — the algorithms I reach for, written
clearly enough to read and reuse.

## What's inside

- **Reinforcement learning:** PPO, SAC.
- **Imitation & inverse RL:** GAIL, MaxEnt IRL, Behavior Cloning.
- **Planning baseline:** MPC.
- Evaluated across CartPole, Pendulum and MountainCar so the trade-offs between methods are
  easy to compare side by side.

It's the breadth behind the depth of `navbot_ppo` — the toolbox that the navigation policy
came out of.
